我們不收集個人資料

本應用程式不會收集、儲存或上傳任何可識別你身分的個人資料。你選取的影片以及分割後的片段，全部都在你的裝置本機上處理，不會傳送到我們或任何第三方的伺服器。

影片與媒體的存取

為了提供分割功能，本應用程式需要在你主動選取影片時讀取該影片，並在你要求時將分割後的片段儲存回你的裝置相簿。這些操作皆在你的裝置上完成，我們無法存取、也不會保留你的任何影片內容。

權限說明

  • 讀取影片權限：用於讀取你選取的影片，以進行分割。
  • 儲存至相簿：用於將分割後的片段存回你的裝置。

分享功能

當你使用分享功能時，本應用程式會透過 Android 系統的分享選單，將片段交給你選擇的其他應用程式（例如 Instagram、TikTok、YouTube）。這些應用程式如何處理你的內容，受其各自的隱私權政策規範，與本應用程式無關。

意見回饋

兒童隱私

本應用程式為一般工具，不特別針對兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

我們可能會不定期更新本隱私權政策。任何變更都會更新於本頁面，並註明最後更新日期。

Thank you for using VideoSplitter (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. We take your privacy seriously.

We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information. The videos you select and the clips created from them are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to our servers or any third party.

Video and media access

To provide the splitting feature, the App needs to read a video when you choose one, and to save the resulting clips back to your device's gallery when you ask it to. All of this happens on your device. We cannot access and do not retain any of your video content.

Permissions

  • Read video: used to read the video you select so it can be split.
  • Save to gallery: used to save the resulting clips back to your device.

Sharing

When you use the share feature, the App passes your clips to another app you choose (such as Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube) through the Android system share sheet. How those apps handle your content is governed by their own privacy policies and is outside the App's control.

Feedback

The App includes a feedback button that opens your device's email app. If you choose to email us, we will receive your email address and message, used only to reply and to improve the App. This is entirely voluntary.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose tool. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
